Common questions

Where is my audio?

On your device, inside Pew Notes. It is not uploaded by default.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings → Pew Notes Pro → Restore purchases, or use Restore on the paywall.

Why are some Spanish readings marked “in revision”?

The offline Spanish text uses the public-domain SBLM draft, which has no imprimatur. The official readings are linked through USCCB.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Export first if you want a copy.
