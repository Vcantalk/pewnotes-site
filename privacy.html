Privacy

Updated October 23, 2026

Short version. Pew Notes is local-first. Your homilies, transcripts, summaries, notes, and intentions are stored on your device. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

What is stored

Audio recordings, transcripts, summaries, intentions, liturgical dates, and preferences are stored inside the app’s container on your device. A small snapshot is shared with the Pew Notes widget through an app group.

Cloud transcription

On-device transcription is the default. If you choose High accuracy or Cloud fallback, the audio for that recording is sent to Apple’s speech service. Pew Notes does not store that audio on a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. Pew Notes does not receive or store payment card details.

Export and deletion

You can export your archive from Settings. Delete all data removes the app’s recordings, notes, intentions, and preferences from your device. Exported copies are controlled by you.

No tracking

Pew Notes does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal data.

Children

Pew Notes is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the Support page or the GitHub issue tracker linked there.
